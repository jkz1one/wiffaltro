This is inactive integrated feature source, not an admitted release or completed
Dynasty mode. It now supports genuine fresh General114 owned run1 or an
authenticated completed Owner112 predecessor continuing into General114 run2.
Both use ordinary Dugout drafting, physical play, pending-round settlement and

## Authority and retained depth

Creation and every independent restore rederive either original fresh genesis
or the exact closed predecessor, selected by an explicit serialized origin.
Fresh genesis uses the original empty-career progress accessors and
`ClubAssetLedger(seed, [0], [], {})`, not standalone grants or `new_club`.
It preserves the original starter-only owned draft pool, empty exotic history,
and prospective pennant/mastery/plaque eligibility metadata without awards.
Current1/one General row is distinct from legacy112 current2/two rows.
The App defaults to fresh only when no predecessor was explicitly provided; an
invalid explicit predecessor rejects without silently starting a replacement.
Existing old save slots are not automatically loaded or overwritten.
Paid Build journals cannot authorize their own starts: inherited progression,
owned recruitment pool, exotic history, predecessor row, ledger, plaques and
pennants must match the authenticated basis. Fourth-pick reconstruction reinstalls
that exact basis. Human/NPC history, called sidecars and pending reports use the
original General validators; no history, rewards or offers are injected.

Ordinary run2 Dugout now delegates the tenth regular round and playoffs to the
unchanged General mechanics, including extra NPC jobs when the human club is
eliminated. Mixed-family terminal receipt/award settlement is integrated but not
positively qualified. Other leagues, Dynasty, subsequent runs, final visuals/audio
and distributable acceptance remain required. Nothing is removed from the plan.

## Integrated persistence and proportional validation

The new version 116 envelope and separate `successor-owned-general-live-v1.json`
slot cannot overwrite predecessor or legacy slots. The 32MiB limit accommodates
retained predecessor evidence plus the active run; exceeding it refuses a save,
never prunes history. The previous 8MiB per-run slot limit is unchanged.

- `Codec.clone` performs one independent reconstruction, rather than capture plus
  a second full restore. Public capture/restore still independently reconstruct.
- The atomic writer validates the existing disk snapshot first, then strictly
  captures the candidate once. Exact full-precision JSON equality and byte
  readback cannot substitute different data for that authenticated envelope.
- Fresh source-aware guard envelopes check candidate/owner immutability without
  replaying the predecessor again. They are equality guards, never authority.
- Original temp-file refusal, primary compare-and-swap, backup preservation,
  readback, busy locks and explicit retained retry remain.
- Successful but unpublished candidates are pinned to both their full envelope
  and durable bytes. Mutation, source change or primary replacement blocks later
  promotion, including after match-cleanup callbacks.

These are removed redundant reconstructions, not a process cache. No measured
latency or integrated positive-gameplay claim is made yet.
