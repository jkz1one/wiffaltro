slot cannot overwrite predecessor or legacy slots. The 32MiB limit accommodates
retained predecessor evidence plus the active run; exceeding it refuses a save,
never prunes history. The previous 8MiB per-run slot limit is unchanged.

- `Codec.clone` performs one independent reconstruction, rather than capture plus
  a second full restore. Public capture/restore still independently reconstruct.
- The atomic writer validates the existing disk snapshot first, then strictly
  captures the candidate once. Exact full-precision JSON equality and byte
  readback cannot substitute different data for that authenticated envelope.
- Fresh source-aware guard envelopes check candidate/owner immutability without
  replaying the predecessor again. They are equality guards, never authority.
- Original temp-file refusal, primary compare-and-swap, backup preservation,
  readback, busy locks and explicit retained retry remain.
- Successful but unpublished candidates are pinned to both their full envelope
  and durable bytes. Mutation, source change or primary replacement blocks later
  promotion, including after match-cleanup callbacks.

These are removed redundant reconstructions, not a process cache. No measured
latency or integrated positive-gameplay claim is made yet.
