## Storage and authority

The origin is one exact116 envelope plus an explicit completed/abandoned closure choice. The frozen reader independently
reconstructs it. Later closed runs are flat `{id, checkpoint, closure}` records, and the
active run has the same shape. A flat checkpoint contains all original durable
season fields except career. Career appears once at the envelope level. Origin,
prior archives and derived start bases never recursively appear in those records.

Each cold reconstruction authenticates the origin, derives the next birth from
its closed career, replays every later run's complete draft, paid Build, physical
and result evidence, then compares the entire regenerated archive and career.
Only the final run may be unfinished. IDs are contiguous; repeated seeds are
legal. A hash, row, structural guard or caller-supplied start basis is not authority.
The original history limit and 32MiB complete-envelope cap reject overflow without
pruning evidence. Hostile primitive nesting rejects before JSON serialization.

Permanent starts, original owned recruiting access and Exotic eligibility come
from the full authenticated closed prefix. Receipt/title rules remain original.
Ledger events and prior awards are preserved, not reset or regranted. New draft
birth is ordinary career drafting, not a developed-roster Dynasty carryover.
Gear-game validation includes the exact preceding Gear-use baseline.
