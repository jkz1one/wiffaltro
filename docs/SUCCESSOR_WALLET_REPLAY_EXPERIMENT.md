# Declined owner-local wallet replay experiment

An isolated candidate removed two duplicate ownership-journal reconstructions
per eligible two-AI Base report. It kept fresh catalog checks, independent later
ownership validation, all original budget/capacity/flow checks, and Doubleheader
reference delegation. Routing around the frozen General114 family required about
333 copied validator lines plus test/routing changes.

An actual detached Dugout game used the unchanged initializer with explicitly
synthetic legal journal funding. Its report passed reference/candidate acceptance,
exact detached-core parity, budget/ownership/capacity/sponsor/recovery/occupancy/
result-link/catalog tampering, fresh-source rejection/restoration and a separate
cold process. This was physical report evidence, not an earned season purchase.
Actual current Standard (two reports) and Doubleheader (four reports) histories
also passed strict restore, report/core/input/resource/save invariance and separate
cold checks. They are controls, not optimized-route timing: Standard has empty
ownership; Doubleheader delegates to the frozen reference. Candidate rejection
of known malformed ownership types was recorded separately, not counted as a
successful reference pass.

The seven-event wallet leaf improved in small samples. A separate 12-cycle ABBA
repeat on that same immutable report measured 24 calls per route: reference
median 0.093060s, candidate median 0.0888335s; means 0.1023243s and 0.0981109s.
Median paired-cycle improvement was about3.6%;10 of12 cycles improved, with large
shared-CPU outliers. Earlier four-call samples showed no reliable whole-report
gain. These are concurrent cloud diagnostics, not player-hardware estimates or
whole-App/save timings.

The extra copied validation surface is not justified by roughly4ms/report while
the principal delay is full-history reconstruction. The candidate was therefore
not adopted. Owner116 gameplay/source and all frozen families remain byte-identical
to `012368b484f3bd86c9f18283941963165cf7a27d`. Audited candidate source, raw timings
and isolated evidence are retained separately; none is represented as a shipped
optimization or a verified full career/Dynasty build.
