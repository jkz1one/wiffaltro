This is a read-only prerequisite, not a next-owner admission, migration or grant.
Every public derive call fully revalidates its supplied terminal112 predecessor
through the exact frozen reader. Caller labels, structural career objects and
returned projections cannot substitute for that replay.

After successful predecessor validation, a private original ClubCareer query
operand contains its rederived completed row and original nonempty asset journal,
pennants and plaques. Its local current=0 selects closed-prefix policy queries;
the archived predecessor current=1 and its envelope remain unchanged. Original
start/close admission routines are not called. The ledger is replayed from the
existing journal, never reinitialized or awarded again.

The result contains detached original-policy Gear/Sponsor progress starts,
Order/Rain/Transfer/Supply and remaining earned ability/access starts, legacy
exotic history and eligible IDs, permanent owned-player access, collection
discoveries, exact asset/award state and the predecessor envelope. It does not
install these into a Build, change a draft pool, create run2, commit an award or
write a save. Future admission must re-run the predecessor and derivation.
