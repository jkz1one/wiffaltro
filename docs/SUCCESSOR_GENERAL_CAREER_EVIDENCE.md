# General114 career evidence adapter

Additive checkpoint-to-career evidence, not owned-season admission. This separate
namespace leaves the original109, terminal112 and General114 engine sources and
catalog descriptors unchanged. New evidence and progress have distinct format
names; no historical record is relabeled or migrated.

The public evidence boundary fully restores the supplied standalone checkpoint,
requires all four picks and no pending physical results, and compares its fresh
canonical capture with the entire input. Restore repeats that derivation and
compares every evidence field. A run ID is a bounded caller label, never proof of
ownership, career ordering, awards or completion.

Current pitch eligibility retains its complete called-sidecar and Doubleheader
proof validation. Development is derived through full General Build transaction
replay and an observer; final book and contracts must match. Historical per-game
rosters remain historical: a fresh recruit does not acquire the outgoing player's
appearances or development. Missing historical provenance remains unknown.
Existing aggregate validation is reused only after explicit current lesson and
eligibility checks. The independent adapter identity binds these adapter files,
the shared validators and the unchanged General catalog.

## Qualification

Godot 4.6.3 isolated Standard and two-game Doubleheader actual earned checkpoints
pass capture, complete replay, mutation rejection and separate-process evidence
reopen. An actual three-settled-round checkpoint with a paid fresh recruit passes
the same warm checks and separate-process reopen. The test checks historical roster/player provenance, altered
identity/catalog/checkpoint/score/progress/sidecar rejection, label bounds,
old/new format separation and unchanged input saves and authored
Resources. A malformed pending injection is explicitly synthetic, not a newly
played pending-game fixture. Runner fixtures are copied read-only into isolated
user data; the only new save is private test evidence.

## Not established

No owned run2, inherited birth, career dispatch, new owner codec, asset grant,
terminal award, Dynasty, completed-season acceptance or distributable build is
provided by this adapter. Its standalone reader deliberately cannot validate
future inherited owner state. Those require their own complete owner replay.
No performance improvement is claimed; these checks retain full validation.

The current detachment assertion mutates an explicit deep duplicate, not the
returned evidence itself; it is not direct alias-isolation coverage of capture.
The capture path reconstructs a fresh season, but that stronger mutation test
remains to be added.
