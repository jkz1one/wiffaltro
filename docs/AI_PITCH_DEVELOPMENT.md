# Paid opponent pitch development

## Selected contract

New Working seasons select opponent policy4 before drafting. The supported common
market adds Pitch Mastery and Round Out to the four stat cards and13 initial passive
Gear. This implements the selected testing contracts from Players v17, Economy v24
and Current Decisions v31, read on2026-10-03. Working values remain Working; no new
balance, tier or content-access approval is implied.

Development keeps parent weight25 against Gear20 across the supported categories.
It uses five equally weighted legal families: Contact, Power, Fielding, Pitching and
pitch mastery. Pitch Mastery/Round Out split that last family equally when both have
legal current targets. Cash, held capacity and buying preferences do not filter
stock. Ordinary last-offer category diversity repair and equal Gear slot subweights
remain; stock never forces a preferred card. The fixed8-Cash pack draws up to three
different eligible families, at most one pitch-level card. It never includes both
mastery variants, lessons or tactical cards, and rerolls cannot regenerate it.

## Objectives and transactions

Before shopping, choose each club's primary fastball from its primary pitcher's
actual active OF/ON/SN repertoire: highest existing mastery, then stable recipe ID.
Eephus and other non-fastballs cannot become a universal primary. Save that exact
choice before the first purchase. The playing/shopping beneficiaries remain the
existing roster roles; a paid upgrade does not retarget them or edit natural assets.

Pitching/defense first goals, in order: primary Pitching4; designated recipe Lv3;
primary fielder Fielding4; secondary Pitching3. Then rotate primary Pitching,
designated mastery, primary-fielder Fielding and secondary Pitching. Keep the cursor
across visits; skip invalid/capped targets. Distributed and featured-hitter objectives
and rotations remain unchanged. Their clubs may see mastery stock and skip it.

Buy an affordable loose card serving the earliest legal objective. For the exact
mastery target, choose available legal Round Out at6 first; it can only raise one
lowest-level active recipe. If the primary is higher than another active pitch,
Round Out cannot redirect the purchase to that other pitch: use available Pitch
Mastery at8 instead. Both upgrade exactly one active recipe by1, with cap5, through
the same shared paid transaction/physical ladder as the human. No teaching, forgetting,
free growth, cap overflow or changes to another player's history are introduced.

A sealed pack is considered only when at least three distinct legal development
families can serve the listed objectives and8 Cash is available. Two pitch variants
count as one family. Do not inspect concealed contents before payment. After reveal,
choose the earliest legal matching objective or skip; fixed choices and legal targets
are preserved/revalidated. Development precedes the prior empty-slot Gear preferences.
Each purchase re-evaluates remaining objectives, stock and wallet.

One own-fixture18/12 W/L reward wallet funds all purchases. At most one ordinary paid
reroll remains allowed, only with no current affordable useful purchase and enough
Cash after the reroll to fund a currently legal useful card/Gear at its actual price.
A target needing8-Cash Mastery cannot be treated as a6-Cash Round Out target. Future
stock and sealed pack choices never determine reroll eligibility.

## Saves, physical matches and disclosure

Policy4/market3 uses Build41 and physical save48; Career21 is unchanged. Save48
explicitly binds policy4/Build41 and requires physical evidence. Save47 remains
policy3/market2, save46 remains physical policy1/2 and historical Build41 score-only
save45 remains unchanged. Old offer/pack policies, receipt IDs and catalog signatures
stay frozen. The decoder chooses the saved policy before replaying partial drafts;
exact replay reconstructs the primary choice, growth, cursor, stock, paid decisions,
wallets and physical requests/reports. Alternate primary recipes, policy/version or
market changes are rejected. New policies cannot use score-only settlement.

Shared committed player definitions carry the purchased mastery into visible and
offscreen physical controllers, preserving personal recipe levels and natural resource
isolation. Existing round projection still settles every fixture/reward once, shops
surviving clubs before human checkout and handles semifinal/final ordering. Human
shopping cannot trigger counter-shopping. Human stock, prospective unlocks, centered
Equipped and immediate live-sale refunds with next-batter effect retirement remain.

Pregame discloses the supported training/Gear pool and a read-only pitching-focus panel
with the committed pitcher/recipe, actual level/cap, Working objectives and both legal
card prices. Purchase history shows the exact recipe, resulting level, actual paid
cost and reason; Round Out at pack price is identified as a pack pick. Existing player
inspection provides the full committed repertoire/levels. The shared themed panels
and wrapping/scrolling keep this disclosure in the existing preparation flow.
